# Waypoint Ink Route — Corrected Medium Scout

**Status:** Proposed for creative greenlight. No renderer, dependency, production asset, or replacement tracer is approved by this document.

**Decision this scout prepares:** Choose the production medium and asset strategy capable of carrying the Ink Route reference bar from the hero through the first Annotation arrival.

**Supersedes:** The SVG-first rendering recommendation in [`technical-scout.md`](./technical-scout.md). That document remains useful only for its behavioral, input, accessibility, audio-synchronization, and performance findings.

## Continuity correction

The tracer at `2e111a9` is a successful **blocking and engineering study**, not a cinematic tracer. It proves that one normalized progress spine can coordinate journey activation, a blank hold, reversible travel, focus handoff, deep linking, mute state, reduced motion, and render-anchored audio without expensive route-frame work.

It does not provide production-representative evidence for material ink, an authored illustrated world, camera travel, layered depth, final sound, or the feeling demanded by the references. Take B is carried only as the director's initial preference for a held-breath rhythm. Its `1280 ms` timing must be screened again after representative picture and sound exist.

The current SVG impact, visible route treatment, map geometry, Annotation composition, procedural foley, and A/B selector are not approved production assets. They may be removed rather than refined. An SVG or spline may still serve as invisible route data, a mask source, or a semantic overlay; it must not set the visible material ceiling.

## Reference bar

The references set a quality bar rather than a layout to copy.

- **Art direction:** A full-frame authored illustrated world in which marks, typography, environment, and product evidence share one visual grammar. Avoid the appearance of a component demo or clean vector diagram.
- **Material:** Ink must pool, diffuse, feather, stain, skip across fibers, and vary in density. The paper must affect the mark. Opacity and scale changes across closed shapes are not representative evidence.
- **Camera:** The route is a traveling subject. Framing must track, reveal, and hand off attention through one spatially continuous world rather than hold a static viewport while UI appears.
- **Depth:** Layered plates, parallax, occlusion, atmospheric marks, focus, and scale create a 2D or 2.5D world with spatial consequence. Literal 3D geometry is optional.
- **Rhythm:** Anticipation, silence, impact, acceleration, breath, and arrival must be directed as an edit. Take B is the current tempo hypothesis, not picture lock.
- **Sound and touch:** A directed wet contact, paper-fiber travel texture, environmental continuity, and arrival release belong beside picture. Sound remains initiated and mutable; haptics may supplement supported touch devices but carry no required meaning.
- **Finish:** The capable tier is judged as a creative master, not as the lowest common denominator. Mobile receives authored coverage, not a cropped desktop canvas. Reduced-motion, muted, low-bandwidth, and enhancement-failure cuts preserve product truth and agency without defining the artistic ceiling.

`The Boat` supplies the ambition for illustrated atmosphere, layered continuous-world staging, camera-led scroll, and audiovisual authorship. The local map-tracer video supplies the Ink Route's specific material bloom, route-led travel, environmental emergence, and destination-discovery grammar. ZERO supplies engineering evidence for a unified narrative spine, production asset preparation, shader-led transformations, warm-path loading, adaptive fidelity, and real-device iteration; its stack is not a prescription.

## What the shot requires from its medium

1. The impact must propagate through representative paper and ink rather than reveal a predetermined blob silhouette.
2. The same authoritative progress value must drive impact development, route birth, layered world emergence, camera travel, Annotation discovery, and synchronized feedback.
3. The world must accept authored raster or vector source layers, masks, textures, and live product overlays without flattening them into one inaccessible video.
4. Arbitrary progress, reverse travel, direct Annotation entry, resize, and mobile recomposition must reconstruct deterministic states.
5. Semantic hero actions, copy, focus targets, and Annotation evidence remain live DOM or have an equivalent accessible surface.
6. Expensive assets and shader variants must be decoded, uploaded, and warmed before their first signature frame.
7. The creative master must support an honest fidelity ladder rather than silently substituting the SVG tracer on weaker devices.

## Credible production routes

The live Treasures categories consulted on 2026-09-01 were `2D Graphics / Canvas`, `3D WebGL`, `Motion Design`, `Video Making`, `Sound`, `Sensory Feedback`, and `Design Editor`. The strongest relevant saved candidates were [CanvasUI](https://canvasui.dev/), [PixiJS](https://pixijs.com/), [Three.js](https://threejs.org/), [React Three Fiber](https://docs.pmnd.rs/react-three-fiber/getting-started/introduction), [Theatre.js](https://www.theatrejs.com/), [PlayCanvas](https://playcanvas.com/), [Spline](https://spline.design/), [Rive](https://rive.app/runtimes), [Remotion](https://www.remotion.dev/), and [Web Haptics](https://haptics.lochie.me/). They are capability candidates, not an approved stack.

### A. Authored 2D plates with a GPU compositor — recommended

**Production route:** Author, generate, acquire, or record paper, ink, cartography, atmosphere, and product-evidence source material; finish it into layered plates, masks, and textures; composite and transform those assets live through a GPU-backed 2D/2.5D scene.

**Fixed in assets:** Paper fibers and stain fields; the authored map vocabulary; environmental illustration; color and tonal grade; checkpoint art; optional ink-flow and absorption textures; selected foley stems.

**Live at runtime:** The ink propagation mask; route progress; camera and parallax; occlusion and reveal; environmental motion; Annotation handoff; semantic DOM; audio gain and rendered-event cues; fidelity selection.

**Renderer candidates:**

- **PixiJS** is the leading implementation candidate because the shot is an illustrated 2D world. Its stable WebGL renderer, texture lifecycle, render textures, blend modes, displacement, noise, and custom filters suit layered plates and material masks without requiring the scene to pretend to be 3D.
- **Three.js with an orthographic or shallow-perspective composition** remains credible when the material study needs a custom ping-pong render graph, deeper camera staging, or CanvasUI-derived ink/post-processing techniques that are awkward to own in PixiJS.
- **CanvasUI** is a curated technique and source-code reference. `Ink Object`, `Canvas`, `Liquid`, displacement, grain, and other effects demonstrate useful shader constructions, but no component should be dropped in as Waypoint's art direction.

**Reference-bar fit:** Highest. It preserves authored illustration while leaving the material, route, camera, and interaction truly responsive.

**Cost and risk:** Requires real asset production, a GPU warm path, custom effect evaluation, and early mobile profiling. The engine cannot compensate for generic or unfinished plates.

**Verdict:** Advance this route to one production-representative tracer after creative approval.

### B. Externally authored performance with live compositing — credible contributor, not the whole experience

**Production route:** Author the impact, environment, or a fixed camera move in Rive, Blender, a motion editor, an image sequence, generated video, or edited footage; synchronize it with live Canvas/WebGL and semantic DOM layers.

**Best use:** A tightly directed impact plate, paper/ink performance, environmental loop, or fixed transition whose art direction is stronger when authored outside the browser.

**Limits:** A flattened video or single baked timeline weakens arbitrary scrubbing, reverse travel, responsive recomposition, branching, deep links, and fidelity control. Rive and Lottie remain strong authoring/runtime candidates for controlled graphic performances, but a vector-only result does not automatically meet the paper-and-ink material bar.

**Verdict:** Keep as an asset source inside Route A or as a distribution cut. Do not choose a video-only or Rive-only page at this gate.

### C. Full spatial Three.js or game-engine world — technically capable, presently overcommitted

**Production route:** Build the map as a spatial scene with 3D geometry, cameras, lighting, shaders, particles, and post-processing using Three.js, React Three Fiber, PlayCanvas, or a comparable runtime.

**Strength:** Maximum freedom for camera movement, volumetric depth, procedural effects, and later exploratory control.

**Limits:** The current reference bar is illustrated and material, not inherently volumetric. Full 3D increases modeling, texture, memory, lighting, responsive-camera, and mobile-precision work and risks turning Waypoint into a generic WebGL spectacle.

**Verdict:** Do not select full 3D by default. Escalate Route A into this route only if the representative camera or depth study proves the 2.5D compositor cannot carry the approved shot.

### D. Custom Canvas 2D world — credible distribution or authored-plate route

**Production route:** Use Canvas 2D, p5.js, or custom drawing with layered raster plates, compositing modes, image sequences, and a deterministic camera.

**Strength:** Direct control, low conceptual overhead, and proven suitability for expressive illustrated sequences such as `The Boat`.

**Limits:** Live diffusion, multi-pass displacement, texture-heavy camera work, and complex post-processing can become CPU-bound or require recreating GPU facilities. Canvas 2D does not become cinematic without authored assets and production direction.

**Verdict:** Keep as a serious fallback and lower-complexity study. Prefer the GPU compositor for the creative master unless a Canvas 2D proof reaches the same bar with a simpler and faster pipeline.

## Deliberate exclusions

- **SVG/CSS as the primary visible medium:** rejected for the creative master because the existing tracer already shows that polished closed shapes and stroke reveals do not prove material ink, layered world authorship, or camera travel.
- **DOM/Motion/GSAP-only choreography:** useful for semantic UI and transitions, insufficient as the world renderer.
- **Off-the-shelf CanvasUI effect as the final direction:** rejected because the component would supply a recognizable technique rather than Waypoint's authored world. Source patterns may be adapted after visual direction is established.
- **Rive or Lottie as a vector-only replacement for SVG:** rejected unless representative dailies prove that authored raster material and world continuity survive the runtime.
- **Flattened video as the only surface:** rejected because the route must remain reversible, responsive, seekable, accessible, and coupled to live product evidence.
- **Full 3D merely to signal ambition:** rejected. The creative master may be GPU-intensive without becoming a 3D scene.
- **Procedural effects without authored assets:** rejected. A renderer or shader sketch is technical evidence until the paper, ink, world, camera, grade, and sound are representative.

## Proposed creative-master production strategy

Use an authored 2D/2.5D world with a GPU compositor:

1. A seamless paper base and tonal stain field establish the substrate.
2. The CTA-origin impact seeds a ping-pong or equivalent propagation mask modulated by absorption and fiber textures.
3. The visible route follows authored guide geometry but is composited through live ink texture, edge breakup, pooling, and dry-brush loss; the guide itself is never the final visible mark.
4. Far, middle, and near cartographic plates enter through the same propagation and camera progress, creating parallax and occlusion rather than independent fades.
5. The camera tracks the route into one representative Annotation destination while live DOM evidence resolves above or alongside the rendered world.
6. Recorded or finished foley stems synchronize to the first rendered impact, positive travel velocity, and checkpoint release. A short supported-device haptic may accompany the arrival only after touch screening.
7. The capable desktop and mobile cuts receive separately composed camera coverage. Reduced-motion presents an authored static chart and direct Annotation evidence; renderer failure presents the useful semantic document.

The leading runtime hypothesis is PixiJS for scene and texture ownership with custom GPU filters. Three.js remains the technical alternative for the material pass or whole compositor if the first effect study demonstrates a clearer render graph. No package should be installed until the medium strategy is approved and the dependency gate is run.

Current authoritative references supporting that hypothesis are PixiJS's [renderer](https://pixijs.com/8.x/guides/components/renderers), [texture](https://pixijs.com/8.x/guides/components/textures), and [filter](https://pixijs.com/8.x/guides/components/filters) guides; Three.js's [post-processing guide](https://threejs.org/manual/en/post-processing.html); CanvasUI's [Ink Object](https://canvasui.dev/docs/components/ink-object) source study; Rive's [web data-binding guide](https://rive.app/docs/runtimes/web/data-binding); and the [ZERO engineering study](https://tympanus.net/codrops/2026/07/17/zero-the-engineering-behind-a-defiant-interactive-narrative/).

## Asset-production and human return path

The strongest route requires authored source material; it does not require Leonardo personally to produce it. The first asset batch can be generated and finished by the production unit, assembled from licensed or recorded material, or created by Leonardo or another artist in an editor. Browser code alone is not an acceptable substitute.

If Leonardo chooses to operate Rive, a paint/motion editor, or another authoring tool, return this bounded package as dailies:

- one `2880 × 1800` desktop master board and one `1125 × 2436` mobile recomposition covering blank paper → impact → first Annotation arrival;
- editable layered source plus separate paper, far-map, middle-map, foreground-atmosphere, ink/absorption-mask, checkpoint, and product-evidence exports with transparent overscan;
- a grayscale paper-fiber/flow texture and at least one ink-density or edge-breakup mask suitable for runtime modulation;
- if motion is authored, a progress-addressable `.riv` file or transparent image sequence with stable first and last states, not only a flattened autoplay movie;
- dry `48 kHz` WAV stems for impact, travel texture, environmental bed, and arrival release when sound is included;
- a short preview movie showing intended camera, edit, grade, and timing, plus source/provenance/license notes.

The production unit screens those returns before integration. If the batch is produced internally, the same package and evidence contract applies.

## Production-representative tracer required after approval

The replacement tracer remains bounded to hero → blank paper → impact → route travel → first Annotation, but it must include representative evidence for every creative claim:

- actual paper, ink, and environmental source assets rather than placeholder geometry;
- live material propagation and visible interaction with the substrate;
- representative camera travel, parallax, occlusion, and Annotation discovery;
- Take B as the starting rhythm, with at least one nearby timing revision only if rendered comparison exposes a real decision;
- synchronized impact, travel, silence, and arrival sound captured with picture;
- desktop `1440 × 900` and authored mobile `375 × 812` creative-master coverage;
- pointer, touch, keyboard, pause, reverse, direct entry, mute, reduced motion, renderer failure, and background/foreground recovery;
- asset transfer, decoded memory, GPU memory estimate, first-use upload/compile trace, worst-frame evidence, and a real mobile GPU session early enough to change the cut.

The creative screening comes first: material, world, camera, rhythm, continuity, sound, and feeling. Engineering evidence then establishes whether the approved picture can ship. The old SVG build may be used as a behavior oracle, not shown as the visual fallback for creative approval.

## Director's recommendation

Approve **Route A: authored 2D plates with a GPU compositor** as the creative-master medium, carry Take B only as the initial rhythm hypothesis, and authorize one replacement production-representative tracer at the existing hero-through-Annotation boundary.

Do not select PixiJS versus Three.js as a taste decision. After the medium greenlight, run a bounded material/render-graph proof using the selected representative assets; choose the owner that reaches the same picture with the cleanest deterministic lifecycle and mobile budget.
