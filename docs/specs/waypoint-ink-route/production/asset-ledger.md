## Generated hero direction

The final image-generation direction asked for a full-body, friendly Thelu on warm Driftwood Paper, wearing the established rust scarf and holding a deep-green leather logbook, rendered as a tactile storybook ink-and-gouache illustration. The composition reserved calm paper around the silhouette so production CSS could crop it across desktop and mobile without inventing scenery inside the bitmap.

The generation source is retained as PNG; WebP is the only large runtime payload. The external references The Boat, Pixel Space, and Active Theory informed pacing, scale, and authored transitions only. No imagery, video, audio, type, or geometry was copied from them.

## Reserved post-production

No video or music asset is required. A professionally recorded dry nib/paper loop and wet ink contact one-shot may replace the procedural bus after device listening, provided provenance, license, edit, peak level, compression, and synchronization notes are added here.
